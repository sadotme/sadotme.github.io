CLUMSY CUBEPRIVACY POLICY

Effective November 6, 2026

Clumsy Cube is a single-player arcade game made by Steven Avila. In short: it doesn't collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things on your iPhone so it can remember them between sessions: your best score, your Music, Sound and Vibration settings, and whether you've finished the How to play lesson. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

No ads, analytics or tracking

This version has no accounts, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. It doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos, and it doesn't connect to the internet while you play.

Children

Clumsy Cube is suitable for all ages. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that changes this, such as ads or online features, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open a request at github.com/sadotme/sadotme.github.io/issues.
