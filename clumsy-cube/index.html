How do I play?

The cube rolls forward on its own. Tap anywhere on the screen to switch which way it rolls. The red arrow on the ground shows where it will roll next. Miss a corner and it falls off the edge.

Every tile you roll onto is one point. There are no power-ups or bonuses, so your score is exactly how far you got.

It keeps getting faster

That's the game. Every so often a FASTER sign appears and the path speeds up and adds something new: forks, dead ends, and quick double turns. Each speed-up starts with a short easy straight so you can settle in. Tap twice quickly to make two turns in a row.

Can I see the tutorial again?

Yes. Tap the gear button, then How to play. The next run will be the guided lesson.

How do I pause?

Tap the pause button in the top corner during a run. The game also pauses by itself if you leave the app. When you resume, a 3-2-1 countdown gives you time to get ready.

My best score disappeared

Your best score and settings are saved on your iPhone only. Deleting the app removes them, and they don't move to a new phone.

No sound or vibration?

Tap the gear button and check that Music, Sound and Vibration are switched on. Also make sure your iPhone isn't on silent.

Does it need the internet?

No. Clumsy Cube works offline, has no account or sign-up, and doesn't collect any data. See the privacy policy.
